Privacy Policy for Chishti Sabiri

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the Chishti Sabiri mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Use

A. Location Information

The app requests access to your device's geographical location (approximate or precise) strictly for the following essential islamic utility purposes:

  • Accurate Prayer Times (Salah / Namaz): Islamic prayer times are calculated based on your current latitude and longitude.
  • Qibla Direction: Calculating the precise compass heading towards the Holy Kaaba in Mecca.
  • Local Weather Information: Displaying local weather conditions and temperatures on the Home Weather Wall header.

Note: Your location data is processed locally on your device or via temporary calculation endpoints. We do not sell, rent, or track your personal movement history.

B. Push Notifications & Device Identifiers

The app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and Supabase to send you:

  • Azan / Prayer time reminder notifications.
  • Important Islamic announcements, Urs Mubarak alerts, and community updates.

We only store an anonymous, randomized device push token. We do not collect personal identities or personal contact lists.

2. Device Storage & Audio Playback

The app may store recitation audio files, Ratheeb offline cache, and book preferences locally on your device for uninterrupted offline usage.

3. Third-Party Services

Our application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android system functionality.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging & Analytics: For push notification delivery and anonymous app performance monitoring.
  • Supabase: For device token sync and announcement delivery.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Chishti Sabiri application is an educational, spiritual, and Islamic utility app suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Security of Your Data

The security of your data is important to us. All communication with notification servers is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.
